常见问题

  • 测不出来：确认已允许麦克风（设置 → Taut → 麦克风），在安静的地方轻敲线床中间几下。
  • 结果和穿线机磅数对不上：Taut 比的是同一根线前后的变化；刚穿好那次勾上「刚穿好」，之后的结果最准。

FAQ

  • No reading: make sure microphone access is allowed (Settings → Taut → Microphone), then tap the middle of the string bed a few times in a quiet place.
  • The result does not match the stringing machine tension: Taut compares the same strings over time. Check "just strung" on the first measurement after stringing, and later results are most accurate.
