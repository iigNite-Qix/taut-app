Taut 隐私政策

生效日期：2026 年 10 月 13 日

Taut 不收集、不上传、不分享你的任何个人信息。

  1. 麦克风：只在你点「拍一下」后打开，用来听线床的声音并在手机上当场算出频率。声音不保存、不上传。
  2. 你填的拍子、球线、测量和打球记录只存在你的手机上，Taut 没有服务器，开发者也看不到。删掉 app 就一起删掉。
  3. Taut 不联网，没有广告，没有第三方统计或追踪。
  4. 以后如果加了需要联网的功能，会先更新这份政策并在 app 里说明。

Taut Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 13, 2026

Taut does not collect, upload or share any of your personal information.

  1. Microphone: it is turned on only after you tap the measure button, to listen to your string bed and calculate its frequency on your phone. The sound is never saved or uploaded.
  2. The rackets, strings, measurements and play logs you enter are stored only on your phone. Taut has no server, and the developer cannot see them. Deleting the app deletes them too.
  3. Taut does not connect to the internet and has no ads, third-party analytics or tracking.
  4. If a future feature needs the internet, this policy will be updated first and the change explained in the app.

Contact: see the support page.
